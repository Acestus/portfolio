Pinewood Derby

Car rules and inspection

  • Cars must meet the size limits and weigh no more than 5 oz.
  • Only official BSA Cub Scout Pinewood Derby kits may be used.
  • Wheel bearings, washers, bushings, and starting devices are prohibited; cars must be freewheeling.
  • All cars must pass inspection at check-in on February 5.

Consult the Pinewood Derby guidelines for the complete rules.
